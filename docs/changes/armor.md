# Armor

Body armor. Shields are in [shields](shields.md).

## Squire Armor

- **Alaron starts in Squire Armor** (`acre:armor/0x0001`), the ACRE plugin's, in place of the original's Leather Armor
  (`aidyn:armor/0x0501`), so his remodel's armoured outfit has armor of its own
  ([ADR 0045](../adr/0045-the-remade-game-is-the-acre-plugin.md)).

  | | Leather Armor (the original's) | Squire Armor (Retold's) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Protection | 2 | 2 |
  | Dexterity | −1 | 0 |
  | Stealth | 0 | 0 |
  | Price | 80 | 300 |

  It uses an original armor picture (`aidyn:image/0x35C3`).

## Looks

- **Armor changes Alaron's outfit.** The remodel wears its armoured outfit while he wears armor that protects, and its plain one
  otherwise; the original draws one model whatever he wears ([graphics](graphics.md#models),
  [rendering](../systems/rendering.md#outfits)).

## In the Party tab

- **Armor is compared.** Armor that protects more than what's worn has a green arrow in the inventory's grid
  ([ui](ui.md#the-party-tab)).
