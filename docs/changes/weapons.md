# Weapons

Weapons' rules are the original's: their damage, hit, range, the skill that wields them and the Strength they need. What changed is around
them.

- **The Archmage Staff can't be replaced.** In the original Niesen can equip another weapon over it, and the staff vanishes with its
  bonuses still on him. Retold refuses the swap ([items](../systems/items.md#equipment), [bug fixes](bug-fixes.md#items)).
- **The Party tab compares weapons.** A weapon that does more damage than the one worn, or as much and more to hit, has a green arrow in the
  inventory's grid; one the member can't wield is crossed out and its card says why ("Needs 18 Strength")
  ([ui](ui.md#the-party-tab)).
- **The details print Min. Strength under its own label**, where the original swaps it with Min. Intelligence
  ([bug fixes](bug-fixes.md#ui)).
- **New weapons**: none yet.
